Backend Developer, Payments Team

We are looking for a backend developer to join our payments group. You will design and build services that move customer money reliably, and you will own them in production.

What you will do:
- Design and ship Python services that handle payment processing, refunds, and reconciliation
- Work with PostgreSQL at scale: schema design, query tuning, migrations
- Build integrations with external payment providers and banks
- Improve observability: structured logs, metrics, alerting on payment health
- Participate in on-call rotation for the payments platform

What we are looking for:
- 3+ years of professional backend experience, ideally in Python
- Solid SQL and PostgreSQL knowledge
- Experience with REST API design and rollout of breaking changes
- Comfort with Docker and CI/CD
- Clear written communication and a preference for simple designs

Nice to have:
- Experience in fintech or payments
- Kubernetes
- Event-driven architectures (Kafka, queues)
